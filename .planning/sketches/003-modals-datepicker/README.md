# Sketch 003: Modals & Custom Datepicker UX

## Design Question
How should modal dialogs, task creation forms, and custom calendar datepickers feel to deliver a high-end desktop app experience?

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/003-modals-datepicker/index.html` in your browser.

## Variants
- **A: Frosted Glass Dialog & Interactive Calendar Popover** — Dark slate modal with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, luminous focus rings, quick-selection date presets (`Today`, `Tomorrow`, `Next Week`), and embedded calendar grid disabling past dates.
- **B: Sharp Minimalist Slate** — Linear-inspired razor-sharp borders (`border-radius: 4px`), high-contrast dark container, and tabbed quick inputs.
- **C: Floating Layered Sheet** — Rounded dialog (`border-radius: 20px`) with distinct visual cards for basic info, priority, tags, and dependencies.

## What to Look For
1. **Interactive Presets**: Click `Today`, `Tomorrow`, `Next Week` to see instant date updates.
2. **Calendar Grid Guard**: Note how past dates (Nov 2, 3, 4) are disabled and dimmed to prevent invalid submissions.
3. **Modal Backdrop Blur**: Contrast between the modal surface and the subtle glowing backdrop.
